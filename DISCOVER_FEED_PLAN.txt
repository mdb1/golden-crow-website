Discover Feed Plan
==================

Purpose
-------

Discover is the app's read-only home feed for curated genetics, rare disease,
bioinformatics, research, education, opportunities, and events.

The feed should feel alive and useful, but the content model must stay compact and
clear. The schema must make a hard distinction between:

1. Root fields that apply to every feed item.
2. Type-specific fields that apply only to one item type.

Common title/body/image/source fields belong at the feed item root. Type payload
nodes should not duplicate those fields.

Schema naming convention:

1. Firestore document field keys must always be camelCase.
2. This rule applies to feed_organizations, feed_individuals, feed_items,
   publisherSnapshot, social child fields, and every type-specific payload object.
3. If planning notes, tickets, chats, or editor copy mention a snake_case field such
   as image_url, source_url, color_hex, description_en,
   is_genetic_report_provider, is_grc_highlighted, product_catalog,
   product_url, call_to_action_label, or show_in_discover_feed, treat that as
   informal wording only.
   The saved key must be imageUrl, imageUploadDataUrl, sourceUrl, colorHex,
   descriptionEn, isGeneticReportProvider, isGrcHighlighted, productCatalog,
   productUrl, callToActionLabel, or showInDiscoverFeed.
4. Do not add new client parser fallbacks for snake_case, legacy aliases, or
   duplicate key spellings. Backoffice writes and mobile reads must share the same
   canonical camelCase field names. The current defensive read exceptions are:
   show_in_discover_feed for the newly requested checkbox, and the payload-node
   fallback described in the type-specific schema section. Writers must still save
   showInDiscoverFeed and the canonical camelCase payload node.
5. Canonical values are different from field keys. Values that are identifiers,
   such as org_genomics_laboratories, pro_clinical_geneticists,
   grc_full_genome, pending_approval, and feed item type values such as
   research_update, remain exactly as defined by their providers and must not be
   converted to camelCase.
6. Firestore collection names, asset filenames, deep-link paths, and other external
   identifiers are not field keys; keep their existing explicit spelling.


Deleted Publisher And Creator Continuity
----------------------------------------

DELETED_IDENTITY_CONTINUITY_CONTRACT.txt is the repository-wide source of truth
for every retained record whose creator, publisher, owner, provider, participant,
or requester identity has been deleted. Its rules are part of this Discover plan,
including for all future feed types and publisher-linked surfaces.

Discover-specific requirements:

1. A valid feed item, event, opportunity, spotlight, saved item, or other editorial
   record remains visible under its own status and retention rules when its
   publisher identity is deleted. Identity deletion must not be treated as a
   parser failure or an implicit content cascade.
2. publisherSnapshot remains historical provenance only. After authoritative
   deletion is confirmed, clients must not render its former name, image, logo,
   icon, color, email, or contact data. DeletedUserProvider supplies the localized
   Deleted user, Deleted profile, Deleted provider, or Deleted organization label
   appropriate to the publisher kind and the neutral gray crossed-out icon.
3. The feed item and event remain tappable and open their normal detail. Tapping
   the deleted publisher attribution opens the provider-owned deleted identity
   state. Active publisher directories may exclude a deleted publisher, but that
   must not remove its retained feed items or saved content.
4. A successful server response confirming the canonical identity is absent is a
   deletion. Offline state, timeout, permission denial, malformed data, or a
   missing embedded snapshot is only unavailable and must use retryable warning
   presentation. It must never be labelled Deleted.
5. The fallback does not widen access, revive archived content, change feed-item
   status, or permit follow/contact actions against a deleted publisher. It also
   does not add identity metadata to any serialized Pocket Genes Object.
6. iOS and Android must have identical state, navigation, localization,
   accessibility, and interaction semantics. Feature tests must prove the content
   stays visible while only publisher presentation and identity-targeted actions
   change.


1. Product Idea
---------------

Discover gives users a recurring reason to open the app even when they are not
actively exploring a genomic report. It should surface useful information from
trusted organizations and individual publishers:

1. News about genetics, genomics, rare disease, and patient resources.
2. Research updates written in plain language.
3. Opportunities such as programs, calls, fellowships, resources, or provider-facing
   content.
4. Upcoming events such as webinars, workshops, conferences, or awareness sessions.
5. Internal Pocket Genes guide cells that point into useful app features.

Discover is not:

1. A user-generated social feed.
2. A public posting platform.
3. An RSVP or application system.
4. A clinical recommendation engine.
5. An organization directory inside the feed itself.

Users can read items, open details, share content, save content, follow
publishers, and navigate to related app areas. They cannot edit feed items or
publishers.


2. Concept
----------

The section is named:

    Discover

Discover is broad enough to contain news, research, opportunities, events, internal
guide cells, and future curated resources without implying that organizations are
the main feed cells.

Important distinction:

1. feed_organizations and feed_individuals are backend publisher records.
2. feed_items are the visible editorial feed entries.
3. Each feed item is published by exactly one publisher.
4. Each feed item stores exactly one of publisherOrganizationId or
   publisherIndividualId and a lightweight publisherSnapshot.
5. Publishers have their own detail screens, opened from publisher areas, not as
   ordinary repeated feed cells.


3. Product Scope
----------------

MVP includes:

1. Five-tab app with Discover as the center/default home tab.
2. Firestore-backed published feed items.
3. Internal local guide cells mixed into the feed.
4. Filters: All, Following, Events, News, Research, Opportunities, plus a "+"
   filter picker for the remaining supported content types.
5. Infinite scrolling and pull to refresh.
6. Empty, loading, offline, and error states.
7. Detail screen for every feed item.
8. Organization and individual detail screens from publisher avatar/name.
9. Generic share.
10. Event share labelled as Invite.
11. Local save/bookmark for feed items, lessons, and genes.
12. Publisher following stored on the user's community profile document.

MVP does not include:

1. User-created feed items.
2. Comments, likes, reactions, or reposts on feed items.
3. In-app event RSVP.
4. In-app applications.
5. Huge custom metadata forms per type.
6. Automated scraping without editorial review.
7. Medical advice.


4. Information Architecture
---------------------------

Main tab order:

    Explore
    Learn
    Discover
    Connect
    Profile

Discover is selected by default on cold start.

Discover screen structure:

1. Navigation title: Discover.
2. Filter row: All, Following, Events, News, Research, Opportunities, and "+".
   The fixed filters remain visible at all times. The "+" opens a single-choice
   modal with all 30 content types. Extra content-type filters are only visible
   in the row while selected; otherwise they are reached through "+".
3. Infinite mixed feed.
4. Local guide cells interleaved with external feed items.
5. Bottom pagination loader and retry footer.

Screen states:

1. Initial loading uses polished skeleton cards, not a blank spinner.
2. Empty external feed still shows local guide cells.
3. Fully empty state appears only if neither external nor local cells can be shown.
4. Offline with cached items keeps the feed visible with a compact banner.
5. Offline with no items shows a full no-connection state with retry.
6. Pagination failure keeps existing items and shows a retry footer.


4.1 Discover List Rendering Contract
------------------------------------

This section describes the exact mobile rendering contract for the Discover list.
It is intentionally separate from the Firestore schema because the same data must
produce predictable list cells, filters, hub entries, calendar entries, and empty
states on iOS and Android.

Core rule:

1. The Discover list is additive. Optional data only affects the UI when that
   optional field is present and valid.
2. The app must not fill missing content with invented facts. Missing cost does
   not mean free. Missing attendanceMode does not mean in person. Missing
   accessibilityFeatures does not mean no accessibility. Missing timeKind does
   not mean a default hour.
3. A malformed feed item is filtered before it becomes a cell. The list should
   look intentional instead of showing partially parsed records.

A. Discover controller and visible sections

Discover is a top-level app tab and is the default center/home tab. Inside
Discover, the app uses a horizontal filter controller, not separate navigation
tabs:

1. Fixed visible filters:
   - All
   - Following
   - Events
   - News
   - Research
   - Opportunities
   - +
2. The "+" control opens a single-choice content-type picker containing all 30
   supported Discover item types.
3. If the selected filter is one of the extra types, that extra type is appended
   to the visible filter row while selected. When a primary filter is selected
   again, the extra type disappears from the row and returns to the "+" picker.
4. Selecting a different filter resets pagination and reloads from the first
   page.
5. Selecting Events again, or opening Discover while Events is selected, triggers
   an automatic refresh so the calendar has the latest event information without
   requiring pull-to-refresh.
6. All and Following can show the trusted publisher profiles access banner.
   Other type filters should not show that publisher hub access banner.
7. The Events filter is special: it renders the event calendar/list section
   instead of the regular mixed feed card stream.

B. Language picker and localized loading

The toolbar language picker controls which authored language is queried and how
supporting labels are localized.

1. Feed items are queried by the selected app language using the feed_items
   language field. Accepted authored values are exactly "en" and "es".
2. Changing the app language clears the loaded feed and reloads the current
   filter for the new language.
3. The app does not translate feed item title, subtitle, body, htmlBody, or CTA
   labels inside the cell. The backend must publish separate English and Spanish
   items when both languages are needed.
4. System labels, empty states, date labels, country names, buttons, and filter
   labels are localized by the app.
5. Publisher profile descriptions can use descriptionEn as the English
   description and description as the Spanish/default description.
6. Country pickers store ISO country codes, but show localized country names and
   flags according to the active app language.

C. Feed data loading and parser gates

The normal Discover feed reads feed_items, then builds cells only from valid,
published documents.

Query rules:

1. Base query:
   - collection: feed_items
   - status == "published"
   - language == selected app language
   - order by publishedAt descending
   - page size 20
2. Type filters add a type condition when the selected filter maps to a
   DiscoverFeedItemType.
3. Events use canonical type "upcoming_event". Newer clients may defensively
   tolerate the legacy value "upcomingEvent" while querying/reading, but backend
   writers must save "upcoming_event".
4. Following does publisher-based queries over the user's followed
   publisherOrganizationId and publisherIndividualId values, chunks the ids when
   needed, then merges, de-duplicates, sorts by publishedAt descending, and
   returns the first page.
5. Publisher recent-item lists apply the same visibility rules as the regular
   feed.
6. The feed list does not fetch feed_organizations or feed_individuals just to
   render a cell. publisherSnapshot is the list-time source for publisher name
   and image.

Parser gates for a visible external feed cell:

1. type must exist.
2. For authored content, type must be one of the supported canonical values. If
   an old unsupported type is encountered, the app may show the
   unsupported fallback only when the root fields are otherwise usable.
3. publisherSnapshot must exist and contain a non-empty name.
4. Exactly one of publisherOrganizationId or publisherIndividualId must exist.
5. title must be non-empty.
6. subtitle must be non-empty.
7. The matching payload node must exist. Canonical writers use camelCase payload
   keys such as upcomingEvent, researchUpdate, and externalArticle.
8. For upcoming_event, upcomingEvent.date is required before it can appear in
   the Events calendar.
9. showInDiscoverFeed defaults to true when missing or null.

showInDiscoverFeed rendering:

1. true, null, or omitted:
   - The item can appear in All, Following, matching type filters, publisher
     recent-item lists, and the Events calendar when it is an event.
2. false:
   - The item is hidden from normal Discover card streams and publisher
     recent-item lists.
   - If the item has type == "upcoming_event" and a valid upcomingEvent.date, it
     still appears inside the Events calendar/subsection.
   - Non-event items with showInDiscoverFeed == false have no Discover list
     surface.

D. Mixed feed composition

The regular Discover stream is a composed list of two sources:

1. External Firestore feed items.
2. Local Pocket Genes guide cells.

The user sees one mixed feed, but the sources are different:

1. External cells come from feed_items and open DiscoverDetailScreen.
2. Local guide cells are app-defined suggestions that open internal Pocket Genes
   destinations such as Learn, Explore, Connect, profile setup, saved content,
   genes, variants, or the publisher hub.
3. Empty external feed does not automatically mean an empty screen; local guide
   cells can still fill the Discover surface.
4. A fully empty state appears only when both external feed items and local guide
   cells are unavailable.
5. Offline with previously loaded data keeps the existing content visible and
   shows a compact no-connection banner.
6. Pagination starts before the user reaches the final rows. If pagination fails,
   existing cells stay on screen and the footer shows retry.

E. External Discover feed cell layout

Every external feed cell uses the same list cell hierarchy regardless of type.
Type-specific content changes the badge, accent, fallback visual, metadata
chips, and detail screen, not the overall cell skeleton.

Cell structure:

1. The entire cell is tappable.
2. Publisher row:
   - circular publisher image from publisherSnapshot.imageUrl.
   - if imageUrl is empty, use publisherSnapshot.imageUploadDataUrl.
   - publisher name from publisherSnapshot.name.
   - supporting label "Published by".
   - type badge with the item type label and type icon.
3. Text block:
   - title from root title, maximum 3 lines.
   - subtitle from root subtitle, maximum 3 lines.
4. Media:
   - root imageUrl is preferred.
   - root imageUploadDataUrl is used only when imageUrl is empty.
   - if neither is present, render the type visual panel/fallback image.
   - media uses a fixed Discover cover ratio and aspect-fill crop.
5. Metadata row:
   - up to 3 short metadata chips.
   - empty metadata values are omitted.
   - chips use the type accent as their tint.
6. Footer:
   - shows the best short date/footer text for the item, or localized
     "Recently" if no footer date exists.
   - shows the generic "Open" affordance.
   - the icon changes when the root sourceUrl exists, but the note-level main
     button remains controlled by sourceUrl/sourceButtonText on the detail
     screen.

Cell color and type styling:

1. The selected filter pill and the cell accents come from DiscoverFeedItemType.
2. News uses a red/pink accent family: primary #E04A5E and end #F9737A.
3. Upcoming events use an indigo/purple accent family: primary #4F46E5 and end
   #7C3AED.
4. Research uses blue/teal, opportunities use red/purple, and the other types
   use the explicit type matrix colors in the client.
5. Organization publisher colorHex affects organization/profile surfaces. It
   does not override the editorial feed item type accent.
6. Individual publisher colorHex affects individual/profile surfaces. It does
   not override the editorial feed item type accent.

F. News rendering in Discover

News is a normal external Discover feed item with type == "news" and payload key
news.

List cell:

1. Badge label: News.
2. Icon: newspaper.
3. Accent: #E04A5E to #F9737A.
4. Title: root title.
5. Preview: root subtitle.
6. Body preview is not shown in the list.
7. Metadata chips, in order:
   - news.category, if present.
   - news.region, if present.
   - publishedAt short date, if present.
8. Cover media comes only from root imageUrl/imageUploadDataUrl.
9. The main CTA comes only from root sourceUrl and root sourceButtonText, with
   the type default CTA used only when sourceButtonText is empty.

Detail behavior:

1. htmlBody has priority over body.
2. body is only the fallback when htmlBody is absent or empty.
3. summary and detailBody are forbidden duplicate keys and must not be read,
   written, generated, or previewed.

G. Event rendering in Discover

Events are authored as type == "upcoming_event" with payload key upcomingEvent.
Do not author type == "upcomingEvent" and do not author payload key
upcoming_event.

Regular feed behavior:

1. When showInDiscoverFeed is true, null, or omitted, an event can appear as a
   regular card in All, Following, and any regular type card stream that includes
   events. The Events filter itself renders the calendar/subsection.
2. When showInDiscoverFeed is false, the event is hidden from the regular card
   stream but remains eligible for the Events calendar.
3. The event card uses the standard external cell skeleton, the Event badge, the
   calendar icon, and the #4F46E5 to #7C3AED accent family.
4. Event metadata chips, in order:
   - event date or event date range.
   - event display time, if timeKind allows a visible time.
   - event location, if present.
5. If timeKind is missing or represents "unspecified", no time row/chip should
   be invented. In particular, the app must not show a default 9 PM or any other
   default hour.
6. Event date display uses the UTC calendar date from upcomingEvent.date. A
   timestamp representing September 15 at 00:00 UTC must display as September
   15, even for users in UTC-3.

Events calendar behavior:

1. The Events filter renders a calendar section instead of the normal card
   stream.
2. The calendar receives only items where type == "upcoming_event" and
   upcomingEvent.date is valid.
3. Event days are computed from upcomingEvent.date using the UTC event calendar.
4. The calendar shades every day on which the event occurs.
5. multiDayLength affects calendar day count only when timeKind supports
   multi-day display: allDay, timeTba, or regionalTimes.
6. If timeKind does not support multi-day display, the event is treated as one
   calendar day even if multiDayLength exists in bad data.
7. Selecting a shaded day shows the events for that exact day under the calendar.
8. Multi-day occurrences use occurrence labels such as "Event title - Day 1" and
   "Day 1 of 3" so tapping any shaded day still opens the same event with clear
   context.
9. Calendar event cards show the date badge, title, subtitle, optional time,
   optional day label, and optional location.

Event detail handoff:

1. The normal note-level main button is still root sourceUrl/sourceButtonText.
   It belongs to every note type and is not part of the event info block.
2. upcomingEvent.actionButtons are secondary event actions inside the event info
   block only. They never replace the note-level main button.
3. Event details use the calm EventInfoBlock layout, grouped sections, and
   omission rules already defined in the event model section.
4. For regionalTimes, the event detail block shows "Revisa el horario en tu
   región." plus the compact regional table when valid regional rows exist.
5. The regional table uses regionalStartTimes, regionalEndTimes, and
   regionalTimezones only. Do not read or save countryDailyStartTimes,
   countryDailyEndTimes, or countryTimezones.
6. maxAttendance null means no attendance limit and renders as "No limit" /
   "Sin límite" where the detail design shows capacity. It must never become
   "max 1".
7. priceMinorUnits is displayed as the authored integer amount, not divided by
   100. If the backend saves 4000, the UI shows 4000.

H. Organization and publisher hub rendering

Publisher profiles are related to Discover but are not ordinary feed item cards.
They are opened from the trusted profiles banner, deep links, spotlight details,
publisher rows, or local guide cells.

General OrganizationsHub:

1. It queries feed_organizations where status == "active", ordered by name, with
   pagination.
2. It queries feed_individuals where status == "active", ordered by name, with
   pagination.
3. The screen uses a segmented controller:
   - professionals.
   - organizations.
4. Professionals render as table/list rows.
5. Organizations render as a two-column grid.
6. Follow buttons use the user's followed publisher ids; unauthenticated users
   see an authentication requirement.
7. The search/filter button opens filters for publisher category and country.
8. Organization filters also support requiresGeneticReportProvider and
   geneticReportCategory.
9. When filters are active, the hub loads remaining pages as needed so filtering
   is applied to the full available in-memory result set, not only the first
   page.
10. Organization isGrcHighlighted affects ordering in the general hub:
    highlighted organizations appear first, but still use the standard
    two-column organization cell there.

GeneticReportCompaniesHub:

1. This is a specialized organization-only surface.
2. It is reached from report-company/provider flows, not from the normal
   publisher segmented hub.
3. It filters organizations by:
   - isGeneticReportProvider == true.
   - selected countryCode.
   - optional geneticReportCategory.
4. The selected country is shown as a flag button. Tapping it opens
   CountryPickerScreen.
5. The default selected country comes from the active app language.
6. Highlighted report companies with isGrcHighlighted == true render first as
   full-width highlighted cells.
7. Highlighted cells use imageUrl/imageUploadDataUrl for the circular publisher
   image and bannerImageUrl/bannerImageUploadDataUrl for the 1024x500 banner.
8. Standard matching report companies render after highlighted companies in the
   normal organization grid.

I. Empty, loading, offline, and retry presentation

1. Initial Discover loading uses skeleton feed cards.
2. Initial publisher hub loading uses hub-specific skeleton rows/cards.
3. Empty All says there are no Discover items yet.
4. Empty Following tells users to follow publishers.
5. Empty Events says no events right now.
6. Empty News says no news right now.
7. Empty Research and Opportunities use their own explanatory messages.
8. Error states keep loaded content when possible and show retry in context.
9. Pull-to-refresh reloads the active feed.
10. Bottom pagination retry only retries the next page; it does not clear already
    visible content.

---------------------

Use three top-level Firestore collections:

    feed_organizations
    feed_individuals

The app primarily queries feed_items. The app should not fetch publisher
collections just to render the feed list.

Only name, status, createdAt, and updatedAt are required. imageUrl,
imageUploadDataUrl, bannerImageUrl, bannerImageUploadDataUrl, and websiteUrl
are optional: omit them or store null when the publisher does not have a usable
image, banner image, or website. imageUploadDataUrl is only a fallback for an
uploaded publisher profile/logo image when imageUrl is empty; it must be a full
data:image/...;base64,... URL and must not be written when imageUrl has a
usable URL. bannerImageUrl and bannerImageUploadDataUrl are organization-only
fields used only by the highlighted GeneticReportCompaniesHub cell when
isGrcHighlighted is true. bannerImageUrl is the 1024x500 banner image source;
bannerImageUploadDataUrl is only a fallback for an uploaded banner image when
bannerImageUrl is empty, must be a full data:image/...;base64,... URL, and must
not be written when bannerImageUrl has a usable URL. These banner fields never
replace imageUrl/imageUploadDataUrl, which remain the publisher profile/logo
image used in avatars and headers. The rest exists for the organization detail
screen and admin context.
productCatalog is optional and organization-only. It belongs only on
feed_organizations; do not store it on feed_individuals, feed_items, or
publisherSnapshot. When present, it must be a real Firestore array of product
objects saved under the exact camelCase key productCatalog. This is different
from taxonomy fields such as organizationType, countryCode, individualType, and
geneticReportCategory, which are comma-separated strings. Each product object
must use only camelCase keys. Required product fields are id, title, and
productUrl. Optional product fields are description, imageUrl,
imageUploadDataUrl, callToActionLabel, createdAt, updatedAt, createdByUserId,
and updatedByUserId. productCatalog.imageUploadDataUrl is only a fallback for an
uploaded product image when productCatalog.imageUrl is empty; it must be a full
data:image/...;base64,... URL and must not be written when imageUrl has a
usable URL. description is strongly recommended and should be at least 30
characters when provided. Do not write product_catalog, product_url,
image_upload_data_url, call_to_action_label, or any other snake_case aliases;
clients do not parse those fallbacks. Product ids such as cat_<uuid> are stable
identifiers and may remain underscore-form identifiers. Compact preview product
cells have a last action row laid out as Open, spacer, and a purchase CTA pill.
That action row must be one horizontal line with all items vertically centered
and a stable fixed row height. The Open label is fixed/localized by the client
("Open"/"Abrir").
callToActionLabel only customizes the purchase CTA pill; if it is empty, clients
show "Buy"/"Comprar". Tapping Open or any non-CTA area of a compact preview
product cell opens a modal product detail screen. Highlighted product catalog
cells do not have an Open label, do not open the modal when tapped, and expose
only the purchase CTA. Product CTA pills are filled with the screen tint, use
light text, and have no outline. The visible CTA pill must be trailing-aligned
inside any width cap; do not center it in an invisible wrapper or add extra
right-side inset beyond the card's content padding. The only exception is the
highlighted product cell, whose main CTA must keep a fixed 16-point/dp inset
from the card's right border. Tapping the purchase CTA pill opens productUrl in
the external browser. On iOS this modal uses a 0.5

5.1.2 Publisher Country Codes
-----------------------------

Mobile clients define country parsing for publishers in dedicated provider
classes:

    iOS:
      DiscoverPublisherCountryProvider

    Android:
      DiscoverPublisherCountryProvider

Canonical storage rules:

    - Field name: countryCode.
    - Applies to both feed_organizations and feed_individuals.
    - Type: optional string.
    - Format: comma-separated canonical keys with no spaces.
    - Valid canonical keys: uppercase ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country codes plus the
      special key GLOBAL.
    - Examples:
      GLOBAL
      US,AR
      ES,MX,CO
    - Store keys only. Never store localized country labels in countryCode.
    - Deduplicate selected keys before saving.
    - Preserve the admin-selected order after dedupe.
    - Omit the field or store null when no country coverage is known.
    - GLOBAL means worldwide or not country-specific and must not be mixed with
      ISO country codes.

Backoffice country picker requirements:

    - Use a searchable multi-select country picker for publisher countryCode.
    - Include a first-class "Global" option that writes GLOBAL.
    - Include all ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 countries as selectable options.
    - Show human labels in the admin UI, but persist only canonical keys.
    - When Global is selected, clear all country-specific selections.
    - When a country-specific option is selected, clear Global.
    - Save selected values as a single CSV string, for example US,AR,ES.
    - Do not save arrays, objects, lowercase codes, labels, or CSV values with
      spaces after commas.

Mobile country behavior:

    - New and edited data must use countryCode only.
    - Do not write countryCodes, countryRegion, country, or region alias fields.
    - The parser splits CSV values key by key, trims whitespace, uppercases ISO
      codes, deduplicates repeated countries, and ignores unknown tokens.
    - The normalized model value remains a CSV string such as GLOBAL,US,AR.
    - Cards, detail chips, and saved items render localized country labels from
      the normalized CSV string.

Root field details:

id:
    Stable item ID. It can be the Firestore document ID and optionally repeated
    inside the document.

publisherOrganizationId:
    Nullable. References feed_organizations/{organizationId} when the publisher
    is an organization.

publisherIndividualId:
    Nullable. References feed_individuals/{individualId} when the publisher is an
    individual.

Publisher ID rule:
    Exactly one of publisherOrganizationId or publisherIndividualId must be set.
    They are mutually exclusive in app logic and backoffice validation.

publisherSnapshot:
    Required for feed performance. Contains only name, nullable imageUrl, and
    nullable imageUploadDataUrl. imageUploadDataUrl is a fallback only when the
    snapshot imageUrl is empty; it must be a full data:image/...;base64,... URL.

type:
    Required enum. Determines filter, badge, accent styling, and which payload node
    exists.

status:
    draft, published, or archived.

publishedAt:
    Required for published items. Main feed sort field.

language:
    Required. Use exactly "en" or "es".

title:
    Main headline for the feed cell and detail screen.

subtitle:
    Short preview for the feed cell.

body:
    Plain-text detail content. Use this for narrative detail, context, links, notes,
    deadlines, instructions, and any detail that does not need structured UI.
    If htmlBody is also present, body is only the fallback and is not the
    preferred rendered detail.

htmlBody:
    Optional rich detail content. Use only when basic hierarchy is needed. The app
    should sanitize and render it. If both htmlBody and body are present,
    htmlBody has priority. Fall back to body only when htmlBody is absent or
    empty.

Forbidden duplicate content keys:
    Do not store, generate, read, or accept an exact key named summary anywhere
    in a Discover feed item document. The only short preview field is subtitle.
    Do not store, generate, read, or accept an exact key named detailBody
    anywhere in a Discover feed item document. The rich detail field is htmlBody;
    the plain-text detail field is body.

    This is strict across news, events, research, opportunities, articles,
    spotlights, guides, stories, and every other Discover type. Do not put
    summary or detailBody at root level, and do not put them inside any
    type-specific payload node. Do not mirror subtitle into summary. Do not
    mirror htmlBody into detailBody. Do not copy the same article text into both
    body and htmlBody unless body is a real plain-text fallback and htmlBody is
    the intentional rich version.

    Clients ignore summary and detailBody if a bad backend document contains
    them. Backoffice validation must block save/publish when either exact key is
    present at root level or inside any payload object.

imageUrl:
    Optional cover image. The app should render it in a fixed 1024w x 500h ratio
    container using aspect fill. This is the preferred portada image field when a
    usable hosted URL exists.

imageUploadDataUrl:
    Optional cover image data fallback for every feed item type. Use only when
    root imageUrl is empty. It must be a full data:image/...;base64,... URL from
    an uploaded portada image. Do not write this field when root imageUrl has a
    usable URL. This is root-level because notes, news, events, opportunities,
    guides, stories, and every other type all share the same feed-item cover
    renderer.

sourceUrl:
    Optional public source, registration, application, article, DOI, or reference
    URL. It is root-level because every type can have one.

sourceButtonText:
    Optional custom label for the detail screen button that opens sourceUrl.
    This is root-level because every item type can link to an external source.
    It must be authored in the same language as the feed item. If missing, null,
    or empty, the app uses its default item-type label from the type matrix below.
    Do not add duplicate type-specific URL fields such as youtube_url,
    spotify_url, note_url, form_url, article_url, tool_url, or application_url.
    The one visible action is sourceUrl plus sourceButtonText.

showInDiscoverFeed:
    Optional root boolean. This is the Discover feed visibility checkbox requested
    as "show_in_discover_feed", but the saved Firestore key is showInDiscoverFeed.
    It belongs at the root of feed_items, next to title, subtitle, imageUrl,
    sourceUrl, and sourceButtonText. It must never be stored inside upcomingEvent
    or any other type-specific payload.

    Accepted values:
        true
        false
        null or omitted

    Default:
        If showInDiscoverFeed is omitted or null, clients treat it as true. This
        preserves retrocompatibility: old feed items display exactly as they do
        today without a migration.

    Rendering behavior:
        true:
            The item appears in the normal Discover feed as before, including
            All, Following, matching type filters, publisher recent-item lists,
            and the Events calendar when it is an event.
        false:
            The item is hidden from the normal Discover feed, Following feed,
            matching non-event type feeds, and publisher recent-item lists.
            If and only if the item is an event with type == "upcoming_event"
            and a parsed event date, it still appears in the Events
            subsection/calendar. The canonical source is upcomingEvent.date;
            newer mobile readers can also recover from the payload node typo
            upcoming_event when upcomingEvent is missing. The calendar renders
            the event on each computed event day exactly like any other event.
            For a multi-day hidden event, clicking any shaded day still opens
            that event, with the day label such as "Event title - day 1" or the
            equivalent calendar occurrence label already used by the client.

    Important:
        This is not an event payload field. It answers "Should this feed item be
        shown in the general Discover feed?" It does not describe the event itself.
        Use showInDiscoverFeed at feed_items/{id}. Do not use
        upcomingEvent.showInDiscoverFeed.


5.4 Type-Specific Schema
------------------------

Each selected type adds exactly one exclusive payload node named with the
camelCase form of the canonical type value. For example, type value
research_update uses payload key researchUpdate, and type value upcoming_event
uses payload key upcomingEvent. The node may be empty, but it must exist so app
clients can prove the item was intentionally authored for that type. Payload
fields describe the post; they do not launch workflows. Videos, podcasts,
surveys, Q&A, tools, and registrations all open through the root sourceUrl CTA.

Canonical writer rule and defensive mobile reader fallback:

    Backend and backoffice writers must save exactly one payload node, using
    the canonical camelCase payload key shown in the type matrix below. Do not
    author snake_case payload nodes and do not dual-write both payload keys.

    Newer mobile clients defensively read the canonical camelCase payload node
    first, and if it is missing, fall back to the canonical snake_case type value
    as the payload node. This applies to every modeled Discover payload type:
    researchUpdate falls back to research_update, upcomingEvent falls back to
    upcoming_event, clinicalTrial falls back to clinical_trial,
    downloadableResource falls back to downloadable_resource, expertQA falls
    back to expert_qa, and so on for all typed payloads.

    This fallback is a compatibility safety net only. The backend contract
    remains canonical camelCase payload keys. Older store builds before this
    compatibility fix still require the canonical camelCase payload key.

    Event-specific key rule:

        type must be "upcoming_event"
        payload object key must be "upcomingEvent"

    Do not save type: "upcomingEvent" for authored data. Do not save
    upcoming_event as the authored payload object key. Newer mobile clients may
    tolerate that payload typo, but backend writers must still emit
    upcomingEvent.

    Correct minimal event shape:

        {
          "type": "upcoming_event",
          "upcomingEvent": {
            "date": "2026-10-12T14:00:00Z",
            "location": "Online"
          }
        }

    Newer mobile clients may defensively tolerate type == "upcomingEvent", but
    that is not the backend contract and must not be used for authored data.

Required field rule:

1. For all 30 types, root title, subtitle, body/htmlBody, language, status,
   publisher, and publishedAt rules still apply.
2. The only required type-specific field is upcomingEvent.date.
3. All other payload fields listed below are optional.
4. Array fields must be saved as array<string>, except
   upcomingEvent.actionButtons, which is array<object>. Do not save
   comma-separated strings for payload arrays.

Upcoming event additive contract:

    Retrocompatibility:

        The existing payload remains valid and complete:

            "upcomingEvent": {
              "date": "2026-10-12T14:00:00Z",
              "location": "Online"
            }

        date is still the only required upcomingEvent schedule field. It is the
        start date anchor. New clients use it as day 1 for multi-day events.
        Old clients continue to show the same single-day date/location UI and
        ignore all unknown optional fields.

    Flat schedule fields:

        date
            Required date value. Start anchor and day 1. Acceptable values:
            Firestore Timestamp, ISO datetime string such as
            2026-10-12T14:00:00Z, or date-only string 2026-10-12.

        Visualización de horario
            This is the backoffice dropdown that decides both which schedule
            fields appear and which schedule fields are saved. Hidden fields
            are not validated and must not be sent in the payload. This keeps
            the model flat, additive, and retrocompatible.

            Sin especificar
                Use when the editor does not want to define a concrete time
                display mode.
                Visible fields: only the Visualización de horario selector.
                Hidden fields: timezone, dailyStartTime, dailyEndTime,
                multiDayLength, regionalStartTimes, regionalEndTimes,
                regionalTimezones.
                Saved payload: do not save timeKind and do not save any
                advanced schedule field.
                App rendering: hide the time row entirely. Do not display a
                default time and do not derive a time from the date timestamp,
                even if date contains a technical hour.

            Evento con horario
                Use when the event has a concrete hour, for example 10:00 to
                11:30.
                Visible fields: timezone, dailyStartTime, dailyEndTime.
                Hidden fields: regionalStartTimes, regionalEndTimes,
                regionalTimezones, multiDayLength.
                Saved payload: timeKind: "timed". Save timezone,
                dailyStartTime, and dailyEndTime only when completed.
                Active validation: dailyStartTime and dailyEndTime must use
                HH:mm syntax, and timezone must be valid IANA when completed.

            Evento de día completo
                Use when the event occupies the whole day without a precise
                hour, for example a full-day conference, campaign, or congress.
                Visible fields: multiDayLength.
                Hidden fields: timezone, dailyStartTime, dailyEndTime,
                regionalStartTimes, regionalEndTimes, regionalTimezones.
                Saved payload: timeKind: "allDay". Save multiDayLength only
                when completed. Do not save timezone, dailyStartTime,
                dailyEndTime, or regional fields.

            Solo fecha
                Use when only the date matters, for example "12 de octubre"
                without hour or duration. This always means exactly one day.
                Visible fields: no extra fields below the selector.
                Hidden fields: timezone, dailyStartTime, dailyEndTime,
                multiDayLength, regionalStartTimes, regionalEndTimes,
                regionalTimezones.
                Saved payload: timeKind: "dateOnly". Do not save
                multiDayLength or any time/regional fields. If multiDayLength
                had a stale value before switching to Solo fecha, it is ignored,
                not validated, and not sent.

            Horario a confirmar
                Use when the date is defined but the hour is not ready to
                publish, for example "12 de octubre, horario a confirmar".
                Visible fields: multiDayLength, plus an informational notice
                explaining that this is for a defined date with time still to be
                announced.
                Hidden fields: timezone, dailyStartTime, dailyEndTime,
                regionalStartTimes, regionalEndTimes, regionalTimezones.
                Saved payload: timeKind: "timeTba". Save multiDayLength only
                when completed.

            Horarios por región
                Use when the event hour changes by country or timezone, for
                example Argentina 09:30, Mexico 06:30, Spain 14:30.
                Visible fields: multiDayLength and a Configurar horarios
                regionales button/table.
                Hidden fields: global timezone, global dailyStartTime, global
                dailyEndTime.
                Saved payload: timeKind: "regionalTimes". Save multiDayLength
                only when completed. Save regionalStartTimes,
                regionalEndTimes, and regionalTimezones only from configured
                regional rows.
                Active validation: every country key must be an uppercase
                ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code such as AR, UY, MX, US, or ES; every
                regional start/end time must use HH:mm syntax; every regional
                timezone must be valid IANA. If regionalTimes is selected and
                no rows are configured, show a review warning.

            Simple rule:
                Evento con horario uses global schedule fields.
                Horarios por región uses per-country schedule fields.
                Solo fecha uses no time fields and no duration.
                Evento de día completo uses duration but no hours.
                Horario a confirmar can use duration but no hours yet.

        timeKind
            Optional enum saved by the Visualización de horario selector.
            Accepted values only: timed, allDay, dateOnly, timeTba,
            regionalTimes. Omit the field entirely for Sin especificar.

        timezone
            Optional IANA timezone string, for example UTC,
            America/New_York, America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires, Europe/Madrid.
            Save only when timeKind is timed.

        dailyStartTime
            Optional HH:mm string in 24-hour time. Example: 08:00.
            Save only when timeKind is timed.

        dailyEndTime
            Optional HH:mm string in 24-hour time. Example: 16:00.
            Save only when timeKind is timed.

        multiDayLength
            Optional integer from 1 to 365. If omitted, the event is one day.
            Save only when timeKind is allDay, timeTba, or regionalTimes. Do
            not save for timed, dateOnly, or Sin especificar. If a stale
            multiDayLength reaches the client for timed/dateOnly/Sin
            especificar, the app ignores it and hides the top-right duration
            label.

        regionalStartTimes
            Optional object keyed by ISO country code with HH:mm values.
            Example: { "US": "08:00", "AR": "10:00" }. Save only when
            timeKind is regionalTimes.

        regionalEndTimes
            Optional object keyed by ISO country code with HH:mm values.
            Save only when timeKind is regionalTimes.

        regionalTimezones
            Optional object keyed by ISO country code with IANA timezone values.
            Save only when timeKind is regionalTimes.

    Event classification:

        eventKind optional enum:
            webinar, conference, workshop, seminar, supportGroup,
            communityMeetup, awarenessDay, course, training, fundraiser,
            clinicalSession, researchSession, networking, livestream,
            recordedSession, other

        attendanceMode optional enum:
            online, inPerson, hybrid, phone, onDemand, toBeAnnounced
            App rendering must always label this field explicitly as
            "Attendance mode: [value]" / "Modalidad de asistencia: [valor]".
            Never render only "[value]" beside an icon, because values like
            Phone/Teléfono are ambiguous without the field name.

        eventStatus optional enum:
            normal, scheduled, tentative, postponed, rescheduled, cancelled,
            completed, soldOut, available_slots

            Exact new status keys:
                normal
                    Neutral/default event state. The app accepts it and renders
                    it like scheduled/no special status row.

                available_slots
                    Means "inscripcion abierta - cupos disponibles". Save this
                    exact key. Do not save the Spanish phrase as eventStatus.

    Capacity:

        maxAttendance is nullable. Canonical payload key: maxAttendance.
        If a backoffice form field is named max_attendance, it must be mapped to
        maxAttendance before saving. The app tolerates max_attendance as a
        legacy read alias, but new payloads should not use it.

        Acceptable values:
            null
                Means there is no attendance limit. Render Capacity as "No
                limit" / "Sin límite". Do not convert null to 1, and do not
                infer "Up to 1 person".

            positive integer
                Means a capped event. Render Capacity as "Up to N people" /
                "Hasta N personas".

            omitted
                Means the backend did not provide the capacity field. The app
                omits the Capacity fact to preserve additive optional rendering.

        Invalid values:
            0, negative numbers, non-integers, strings, arrays, and objects.

    Event actions / Acciones del evento:

        actionButtons is an optional array of button objects. Use this instead
        of flat registrationUrl/accessUrl/agendaUrl/materialsUrl fields.

        Important hierarchy rule:
            actionButtons are Acciones del evento: event-scoped secondary
            actions that render only inside the upcomingEvent detail block, in
            the Acciones del evento subsection.

            actionButtons are not the Botón principal. The main note button is
            the existing root-level/global CTA for every Discover note type,
            controlled by the shared fields such as sourceUrl and
            sourceButtonText. Do not move the main CTA into upcomingEvent and do
            not use actionButtons as a replacement for the main CTA.

        Button object:
            type
                Required enum: register, join, learnMore, viewAgenda,
                watchRecording, downloadMaterials, contactOrganizer.
                One button per type maximum. Clients keep the first valid
                button of a type and ignore later duplicates.

            title
                Optional string, max 56 characters. If omitted, clients use the
                default title for the type.

            url
                Required URL string. Use https:// for web destinations.
                contactOrganizer may use mailto:.

        Default titles:
            register -> Register
            join -> Join event
            learnMore -> Learn more
            viewAgenda -> View agenda
            watchRecording -> Watch recording
            downloadMaterials -> Download materials
            contactOrganizer -> Contact organizer

    Organizer and relationship:

        publisherRelationshipToEvent optional enum:
            organizer, coOrganizer, speaker, sponsor, partner, participant,
            attendee, mentioning, unknown

        organizerName
            Optional string, max 80 characters. This is the only organizer
            identity field accepted inside upcomingEvent. Do not add
            organizerOrganizationId or organizerIndividualId to upcomingEvent.

        publisherDisclosure
            Optional string, max 140 characters.

    Audience, cost, language, and accessibility:

        audience optional array:
            patients, families, caregivers, students, clinicians, researchers,
            geneticCounselors, advocates, industry, generalPublic

        costType optional enum:
            free, paid, donation, varies, unknown

        currency
            Optional ISO 4217 code. Dependency: if costType is free, currency is
            never used and not required. If costType is paid, currency can still
            be empty and the app renders "Paid" without a price.

        priceMinorUnits
            Optional integer >= 0. Dependency: if costType is free,
            priceMinorUnits is never used and not required. If costType is paid,
            priceMinorUnits can still be empty and the app renders "Paid"
            without a price. Important display rule: despite the legacy field
            name, clients render this integer as the authored price amount
            without reducing cents/minor units. Example: priceMinorUnits=4000
            renders as 4000, not 40.00.

        languages
            Optional language-code array such as en, es, pt-BR.

        accessibilityFeatures optional array:
            captions, liveTranscript, signLanguage, wheelchairAccessible,
            recordingAvailable, quietRoom, translatedMaterials

    Rendering:

        Detail UI remains additive. Rows render only for valid present fields.
        A legacy event still shows the current Date, Time, Location, and Max
        attendance rows.

        The calendar derives visible days from date + multiDayLength only when
        timeKind is allDay, timeTba, or regionalTimes. There is no
        occurrences[] array and no multi-session model. If multiDayLength=3 on
        an accepted multi-day mode, the calendar shades three days. Clicking
        day 2 shows "Event title - Day 2" and a "Day 2 of 3" chip, while
        opening the same underlying event.

        Schedule rendering follows timeKind:
            Omitted timeKind / Sin especificar: render the date and location
            normally, but hide the time row entirely. Do not show a default
            hour, and do not derive a visible hour from the date timestamp.
            timed: dailyStartTime and dailyEndTime render as one concrete
            single-day schedule. If only dailyStartTime exists, render "Starts
            HH:mm". If both exist, render "HH:mm-HH:mm". Do not show the
            top-right duration label for timed.
            allDay: render "All day"; do not render timezone or hour rows.
            dateOnly: render the date only and no time row.
            timeTba: render "Time TBA" and duration when present.
            regionalTimes: render the relevant regional row when the client can
            choose one; otherwise render a compact "Times vary by region" hint.
            In the event detail view, regionalTimes also renders a compact table
            under the schedule/location group when at least one country has both
            a valid regionalStartTimes entry and a valid regionalEndTimes entry.
            The table has exactly three visual columns: country flag, start
            time, and duration. Duration is computed by the client from the
            difference between the country start and end clocks and is shown in
            simplified text such as 30m, 1 hour, or 1h 30m. Countries without a
            computable start/end pair do not create placeholder rows.
            Do not fall back to global timezone/dailyStartTime/dailyEndTime for
            newly authored regionalTimes events because those fields are not
            saved with that mode.

        actionButtons render only when valid entries are present, and only as
        secondary buttons inside the upcomingEvent Acciones del evento
        subsection. If no valid actionButtons exist, that subsection does not
        appear unless virtualMeetingLink is present.

        If virtualMeetingLink is present and there is no valid join action, the
        app adds a secondary "Join virtual meeting" action inside the same event
        subsection. If a join button exists, virtualMeetingLink is suppressed to
        avoid duplicate join actions.

        sourceUrl/sourceButtonText keep rendering as the global Botón principal
        for the whole note. This global CTA appears outside the event block and
        is never suppressed just because actionButtons exist.

    Justification:

        The model stays flat so event creation remains simple and
        retrocompatible. Authors can still fill only date and location. Richer
        fields are optional, strict, and deterministic, which lets the app add
        multi-day calendar shading, daily hours, regional time hints, cost,
        audience, organizer context, and typed links without cluttering legacy
        UI or requiring a nested occurrence/session system.

    Sample:

        "upcomingEvent": {
          "date": "2026-10-12T00:00:00Z",
          "location": "Boston",
          "timeKind": "regionalTimes",
          "multiDayLength": 3,
          "regionalStartTimes": {
            "US": "08:00",
            "AR": "10:00"
          },
          "regionalEndTimes": {
            "US": "16:00",
            "AR": "18:00"
          },
          "regionalTimezones": {
            "US": "America/New_York",
            "AR": "America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires"
          },
          "eventKind": "conference",
          "attendanceMode": "inPerson",
          "eventStatus": "scheduled",
          "actionButtons": [
            {
              "type": "register",
              "title": "Register now",

5.6 Canonical Key Contract
--------------------------

All new and edited Discover records must use only the camelCase field keys in
this document. Do not write snake_case aliases, do not dual-write legacy names,
and do not add mobile parser fallbacks for alternate spellings.

Examples:

    imageUrl, not image_url
    imageUploadDataUrl, not image_upload_data_url
    bannerImageUrl, not banner_image_url
    bannerImageUploadDataUrl, not banner_image_upload_data_url
    sourceUrl, not source_url
    sourceButtonText, not source_button_text
    showInDiscoverFeed, not show_in_discover_feed
    htmlBody, not html_body
    researchUpdate, not research_update, when naming the authored payload object key
    upcomingEvent.date, not upcoming_event.date, when authoring event payloads
    colorHex, not color_hex
    descriptionEn, not description_en
    isGeneticReportProvider, not is_genetic_report_provider
    geneticReportCategory, not genetic_report_category
    isGrcHighlighted, not is_grc_highlighted
    productCatalog, not product_catalog
    productUrl, not product_url
    callToActionLabel, not call_to_action_label

Canonical values remain exact values:

    type: "research_update"
    type: "upcoming_event"
    organizationType: "org_genomics_laboratories"
    individualType: "pro_clinical_geneticists"
    status: "pending_approval"
    geneticReportCategory: "grc_full_genome"
    geneticReportCategory: "grc_reproductive,grc_full_genome"

Event naming must not mix these two concepts:

    Root type value:
        Use "upcoming_event".
        Do not use "upcomingEvent".

    Payload object key:
        Use upcomingEvent.
        Do not use upcoming_event when authoring data. Newer mobile clients may
        read it only as a defensive fallback when upcomingEvent is missing.

If someone asks for a snake_case field in a ticket or conversation, translate it
to the corresponding camelCase saved key before implementing or entering data.


5.7 Validation Rules
--------------------

Before publishing:

Root:

1. Exactly one of publisherOrganizationId or publisherIndividualId must exist.
2. publisherSnapshot.name must be non-empty.
3. type must be supported.
4. title must be non-empty.
5. subtitle must be non-empty.
6. body must be non-empty unless htmlBody is present.
7. htmlBody, if present, must be sanitized.
8. imageUrl, if present, must be a valid HTTPS URL.
9. root imageUploadDataUrl, if present, must be a full
   data:image/...;base64,... URL and only used when root imageUrl is empty. This
   is the portada/cover fallback for all feed item types. Do not put cover image
   data inside notes, events, or any other payload object.
10. publisherSnapshot.imageUploadDataUrl, if present, must be a full
   data:image/...;base64,... URL and only used when publisherSnapshot.imageUrl
   is empty.
11. feed_organizations.bannerImageUrl, if present, must be a valid HTTPS image
    URL and is used only for highlighted GRC company banner media.
12. feed_organizations.bannerImageUploadDataUrl, if present, must be a full
    data:image/...;base64,... URL and only used when bannerImageUrl is empty.
13. sourceUrl is empty/null or a valid HTTPS URL.
14. sourceButtonText is empty/null or a short action label in the same language
    as the item. It only affects the button that opens sourceUrl.
15. publishedAt must exist.
16. language must be "en" or "es".
17. status must be draft, published, or archived.
18. showInDiscoverFeed must be true, false, null, or omitted. It is root-level
    only. If omitted or null, the app treats it as true.

Organization productCatalog:

1. productCatalog, when present, must be an organization-only array of product
   objects. It must not be saved on individuals, feed items, or snapshots.
2. Each product object must have non-empty id, title, and productUrl.
3. productUrl must be a valid HTTP/HTTPS URL because the product purchase CTA
   opens it directly in the external browser.
4. description is optional but strongly recommended and should be at least 30
   characters when provided.
5. imageUrl is optional; when present, it must be a valid HTTPS image URL.
6. imageUploadDataUrl is optional and must be used only when imageUrl is empty.
   It must be a full data:image/...;base64,... URL for the uploaded product
   image. Do not write imageUploadDataUrl when imageUrl has a usable URL.
7. callToActionLabel is optional and should be a short action label. It only
   changes the purchase CTA pill and the bottom CTA in the product detail
   modal; it never changes the fixed Open/Abrir label used by compact preview
   cells. If it is empty, clients show "Buy"/"Comprar".
8. Product object keys must be camelCase only, for example productUrl,
   imageUploadDataUrl, and callToActionLabel, never product_url,
   image_upload_data_url, or call_to_action_label.

Payload:

1. exactly one matching payload node must exist.
2. non-matching payload nodes must be absent.
3. payload must not duplicate root common fields.
4. upcomingEvent.date must exist.
5. upcomingEvent.maxAttendance must be null or a positive integer. Null means
   no attendance limit and renders as "No limit" / "Sin límite"; it must never
   be coerced to 1.
6. all other payload fields are optional but, when present, must use the field
   type defined in the type matrix.
7. featuredOrganizationId must reference an existing active
   feed_organizations document when present.
8. featuredIndividualId must reference an existing active feed_individuals
   document when present.


6. Feed Querying and Pagination
-------------------------------

MVP query:

    feed_items
    where status == "published"
    order by publishedAt descending
    limit pageSize

Recommended page size:

    20

After fetching and parsing backend documents, app clients apply root
showInDiscoverFeed:

    missing/null -> true
    true         -> visible in normal Discover feed surfaces
    false        -> hidden from normal Discover feed surfaces

This is a client-side visibility rule, not a new Firestore query field. No new
index is required for showInDiscoverFeed. The Events subsection/calendar is the
only exception: event documents with type == "upcoming_event" and a parsed event
date remain visible in Events when showInDiscoverFeed is false. The canonical
source is upcomingEvent.date; newer mobile readers can also recover from the
payload node typo upcoming_event when upcomingEvent is missing. This lets the
backoffice create calendar-only event entries without creating a normal feed
card.

Filter query examples:

All:

    status == published
    orderBy publishedAt desc

Events:

    status == published
    type == upcoming_event
    orderBy publishedAt desc

News:

    status == published
    type == news
    orderBy publishedAt desc

Research:

    status == published
    type == research_update
    orderBy publishedAt desc

Opportunities:

    status == published
    type == opportunity
    orderBy publishedAt desc

Recommended Firestore indexes:

1. feed_items: status ascending, publishedAt descending.
2. feed_items: status ascending, type ascending, publishedAt descending.
3. feed_organizations: status ascending, name ascending.
4. feed_individuals: status ascending, name ascending.

3. Published at.
4. Language: en or es.

6. Subtitle.
7. Body.
8. Optional HTML body.
9. Image URL or upload.

1. The editor label can say show_in_discover_feed if the team wants that exact

2. This checkbox is a root field on feed_items, not part of upcomingEvent.
3. Default checked. If the saved field is absent or null, mobile clients treat it
   as true.
4. Checked / true: the item appears in the normal Discover feed surfaces.
5. Unchecked / false: the item is hidden from normal Discover feed surfaces. For
   event posts only, it still appears in the Events subsection/calendar.

Type-specific fields:

1. The Type selector offers the 30 canonical values from section 5.4.

3. The payload group contains only the optional fields listed in the matrix.
4. upcomingEvent.date is the only required payload field.
5. organizationSpotlight shows a selector for featuredOrganizationId.
6. professionalSpotlight shows a selector for featuredIndividualId.
7. No type-specific external URL fields are shown. sourceUrl is the single CTA

1. Tags.
2. Locale or languageCode.

7. Key points.

9. Video, podcast, survey, form, article, tool, registry, or application URL
    duplicates when sourceUrl can cover them.
10. Deadline/application URL duplicates when sourceUrl/body can cover them.
11. Type-specific CTA label duplicates when sourceButtonText can label the
    sourceUrl button.

14. Content Quality and Medical Safety
--------------------------------------

Discover can include health-related and research-related content, so editorial tone
matters.

Rules:

1. Do not publish individualized medical advice.
2. Do not imply diagnosis or treatment recommendations from a general feed item.
3. Research updates should use educational language.
4. Source links can be included in sourceUrl and body/htmlBody.
5. If sourceUrl needs a custom CTA label, use sourceButtonText.
6. Avoid sensational wording around cures, risk, prognosis, or genetic findings.
7. For patient-facing content, use clear plain language.

   row opens a secondary picker screen.
5. Secondary picker screens opened from the Search organizations/Search
   professionals modal are plain pushed screens, not colored header cards. They
   have no Apply CTA and no option-box/check-circle accessory; tapping one row
   immediately writes that filter value and returns to the main search modal.
6. Professional filters are publisher category and country. Each is
   single-choice.
7. Organization filters are publisher category, country,
   isGeneticReportProvider, and geneticReportCategory. Publisher category,
   country, and geneticReportCategory are single-choice. The
   geneticReportCategory picker appears only when isGeneticReportProvider
   is checked.
8. Organization category filters use only the fixed org_ category keys.
9. Professional category filters use only the fixed pro_ category keys.
10. Country filters use ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes. The CountryPickerScreen shows
   localized country names, a small flag icon, a right-side letter picker, and
   promotes language-specific priority countries before the alphabetized list.
   The flag icon background is clear so the native flag colors stay visible.
   When the selected app language is English, priority countries are New
   Zealand (NZ), United States (US), Australia (AU), and United Kingdom (GB),
   in that order. When the selected app language is Spanish, priority countries
   are Argentina (AR), Spain (ES), Uruguay (UY), Chile (CL), and Mexico (MX),
   in that order. These priority choices only affect mobile picker ordering;
   saved filters still use ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes.
11. geneticReportCategory filters use only OrganizationReportCategoryProvider:
    grc_reproductive, grc_ophthalmics, grc_full_genome,
    grc_cardiovascular, grc_rare_diseases, grc_neurological, grc_prenatal,
    grc_nutrition_and_metabolism, grc_ancestry, grc_hereditary_cancer, and
    grc_other. grc_other is a normal report category and must be listed with the
    other selected categories, not treated as a fallback.
12. Filtering parses publisher organizationType, individualType, and
   geneticReportCategory as comma-separated canonical keys and matches key by
   key.
13. When any trusted-publisher filter is active, the app loads remaining
   publisher pages so the filter can evaluate the full paginated publisher list.
14. Only active organizations and active professionals appear in mobile publisher
   listings; pending_approval, inactive, and archived publishers do not appear.
15. Organization list cells show the isGeneticReportProvider boolean as a
    natural localized row when true. They do not concatenate
    geneticReportCategory values into the compact grid cell.
16. Organization detail screens show a "Product catalog" section immediately
    above Recent updates when productCatalog contains products. The detail
    section is fully opt-in: if productCatalog is absent, null, or empty, there
    must be no product block, no section title, no placeholder, and no empty
    state. When products exist, the section is a one-row hint using two-column
    ProductGridCell cards, shows only the first two products, and renders a
    small gray product description capped at three lines. It always shows a
    "View all" link whenever at least one product exists, even if there is only
    one product. The full Product catalog screen uses one-per-row highlighted
    product cells. Compact product cells end with an action row laid out as
    Open/Abrir, spacer, and a purchase CTA pill. That row must remain one
    horizontal line with all items vertically centered and no vertical drift.
    Highlighted product cells have no Open/Abrir label and no cell tap action;
    only their purchase CTA is
    interactive. The Open/Abrir label is fixed by the client. callToActionLabel
    only customizes the purchase CTA pill and the bottom CTA in the product
    detail modal; empty values fall back to Buy/Comprar. Tapping Open/Abrir or
    any non-CTA area of a compact preview product cell opens a modal product
    detail screen with the full product information shown at larger sizes and a
    large bottom CTA pinned to the sheet bottom. iOS must use
    presentationDetents([.fraction(0.5)]), and Android must use the equivalent
    half-height bottom sheet. Tapping the purchase CTA opens productUrl in the
    external browser. This behavior applies to organization publishers only.
    Compact preview ProductGridCell images are square thumbnails sized to one
    quarter of the actual cell width, clipped, and placed in their own top row
    aligned left with trailing space. A flexible vertical spacer sits between
    that thumbnail row and the title. Title, description, and the action row sit
    below that spacer and use the full cell width. Images must never occupy the full card width or
    overlap text/actions.
    Highlighted product catalog cells keep their larger media treatment.
    Product CTA pills are inverted/filled: tint background, light text, no
    outline. The visible pill must be trailing-aligned inside any width cap and
    must not sit inside extra invisible right-side space. Highlighted product
    cells are the explicit exception: their main CTA keeps a fixed 16-point/dp
    right inset from the card border.
17. SuggestedProvidersInformationScreen consumes OrganizationReportCategoryProvider
    for all supported grc_* cards. Tapping a card opens GeneticReportCompaniesHub
    directly, skipping the pushed country picker. The default country is Argentina
    for Spanish and New Zealand for English. GeneticReportCompaniesHub shows the
    selected country flag as a top-right button; tapping it presents
    CountryPickerScreen modally so the user can switch the single selected country
    and dismiss back into the hub. The hub is an organization-only company surface
    filtered with isGeneticReportProvider == true, the selected geneticReportCategory
    key, and the selected country code. Because
    geneticReportCategory is stored as a comma-separated string, a company with
    grc_reproductive,grc_full_genome appears from both the reproductive and full
    genome paths. This surface is not a publisher hub: it has no professional
    segment, no search/filter button, no magnifying-glass icon, no publisher
    wording, and no "Show all" fallback when no company is found. Its empty
    state apologizes that no company providing this service has been found in
    the selected region yet and says the network is being expanded.
18. GeneticReportCompaniesHub splits matching companies into highlighted and
    standard groups. Highlighted organizations have isGrcHighlighted == true and
    render first as full-width one-per-row OrganizationsHubHighlightedGridCell
    rows. The highlighted cell has a Discover-feed-like header using
    imageUrl/imageUploadDataUrl as the circular publisher profile image, then
    uses bannerImageUrl as the 1024x500 banner media. If bannerImageUrl is empty
    and bannerImageUploadDataUrl contains a full data:image/...;base64,... URL,
    the highlighted banner uses that upload-data URL as its fallback. Standard
    organizations render afterward in the normal two-column OrganizationsHubGridCell
    grid.
    The general OrganizationsHub organization tab also honors isGrcHighlighted,
    but only as ordering: highlighted organizations appear first in the standard
    two-column OrganizationsHubGridCell grid and do not use the full-width
    highlighted cell there.


19. Recommended MVP Scope
-------------------------

The smallest useful version:

1. feed_organizations.
2. feed_individuals.
3. feed_items.
4. Root common feed item fields.
5. Small type-specific payload nodes.
6. Thirty supported types listed in section 5.4.
7. Discover feed with external and local cells.
8. Infinite scroll.
9. Type filters.
10. Detail screen.
11. Organization and individual publisher detail screens.
12. Share/save/follow actions.

What to defer:

1. Large structured metadata forms.
2. Tags and personalization.
3. Search.
4. Push notifications.
5. User reactions.
6. In-app RSVP/application.
7. Automated scraping.


18. Final Product Shape
-----------------------

Discover should feel like this:

1. A user opens the app.
2. The first screen shows a clean stream of useful genetics and rare disease
   information.
3. The user can scan content by type.
4. The user sees who published every item.
5. The user opens details and reads a clear body.
6. Source links are root-level and work consistently across types.
7. Events show date, location, and attendance cap when available.
8. Opportunities show type, requirements, eligibility, and location.
9. Richer hierarchy comes from htmlBody, not dozens of separate fields.
10. The app remains read-only and credible.
