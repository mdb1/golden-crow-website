Atomic banner kit

This is an assembly example, not a flattened banner or a phone image. Every casing, screen, card, glyph, surface, reflection and shadow is a separate layer. All text is editable.

What is editable: the company name, title, description, labels, prices, accent color, and every primitive position. Nothing inside the SVG assets contains a brand, label, price or phone screen. The default source accent is saturated magenta; the renderer replaces currentColor directly, leaving white highlights and neutral hardware intact. The logical canvas is always 1024 × 500.
